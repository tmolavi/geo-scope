## Executive Summary

When billions of citizens around the world ask generative AI engines questions about their careers, skill development, migration pathways, financial security, and business formation, what answers do they receive?

The **Global AI Answers Benchmark 2026.2** represents the largest open-source empirical study to date measuring generative AI responses across **50 countries**, **22+ native languages**, and **9 core human concern categories**.

## Core Findings for Industry & Policy

### 1. The Grounding Divide: Answer Engines vs. Language Models
Search-grounded answer engines (Perplexity Sonar, Gemini Search) frequently cite official government resources (`gov.uk`, `make-it-in-germany.com`, `canada.ca`) and live documentation when answering migration and legal questions. In contrast, pure LLMs generate generalized institutional advice.

### 2. Digital Nomad & Migration Visibility Clusters
Across inquiries in Latin America, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East, Germany (Chancenkarte/Blue Card), Canada (Express Entry), and the United Arab Emirates (Golden Visa) emerge as the most consistently visible migration pathways across all four models.

### 3. Open-Source vs. Commercial Framework Visibility
In artificial intelligence adoption inquiries, open-source frameworks (Python, PyTorch, Docker, LangChain, CrewAI) and open model architectures (Llama, DeepSeek) exhibit widespread organic mention across both English and non-English inquiries.
