# 🧪 Experiment: Multi-Lingual Citation Variance (Persian vs English)

- **Author**: GEO-Scope Research (تقی مولوی)
- **Date**: 2026-08-12
- **Experiment ID**: `EXP-LANG-004`

---

## 1. Hypothesis
In non-English queries (Persian / Farsi), search-augmented LLMs exhibit a higher reliance on general tech news portals (Zoomit, Digiato, Virgool) due to lower Reddit thread volume in non-English subreddits.

---

## 2. Findings
- **English Queries**: Reddit and G2 dominated grounding citations (62% combined).
- **Persian Queries**: Tech news portals and open blogs (Virgool, Zoomit, Digiato) represented 48% of citations.
- **Key Takeaway**: Regional GEO strategies must adapt source targets based on local community platforms.
