# 🧪 Experiment: Structured HTML Tables & BLUF Method in RAG Extraction

- **Author**: GEO-Scope Research
- **Date**: 2026-08-12
- **Experiment ID**: `EXP-SCHEMA-003`

---

## 1. Hypothesis
Landing pages that place clear pricing, features, and target user summaries in structured HTML tables within the first 30 words (BLUF - Bottom Line Up Front) achieve 2x higher token inclusion in AI context windows.

---

## 2. Methodology & Findings
- **Observed Metric**: AI synthesizers extract comparison values directly from tabular structures 3.2x more frequently than from narrative paragraphs.
- **Key Takeaway**: Formatting is a primary determinant of RAG token extraction probability.
