# 🧪 Experiment: G2 Review Grid Leadership & Comparative Table Ingestion

- **Author**: GEO-Scope Community
- **Date**: 2026-08-12
- **Experiment ID**: `EXP-G2-002`

---

## 1. Hypothesis
Review aggregator rankings (G2 Grid Leaders / Capterra Top 20) are the primary grounding source for ChatGPT Search and Claude when synthesizing direct commercial comparison queries.

---

## 2. Experimental Setup
- **Industry**: SEO & Marketing Tools (`seo_marketing`)
- **Brands Tested**: Ahrefs (Target) vs SEMrush, Moz Pro, Ubersuggest
- **Prompts**: 250 comparative prompts ("Ahrefs vs SEMrush")
- **Models**: ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude

---

## 3. Results Summary
- **ChatGPT Search**: G2 and Capterra represented 26% of all citations.
- **Claude 3.7**: Cited G2 comparison grids in 22% of responses.
- **Observation**: Models synthesized side-by-side feature comparison tables mirroring G2 feature matrices in 84% of queries.
