# LinkedIn Post Draft: Launching the GEO, SEO & Digital Marketing Agency Iran 2026 Benchmark

🚀 Excited to publish our latest empirical research milestone: **GEO, SEO & Digital Marketing Agency Iran 2026 Benchmark (v2026.1)**.

When potential clients ask AI assistants like Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity for agency recommendations in Iran (e.g. Web24, Novin, Dimarketing, Triboon, DMN Agency), what answers do these models actually generate?

Rather than guessing or claiming to "reverse-engineer secret AI ranking algorithms", we built an open, scientific pipeline to measure observed visibility:

🔹 **AnswerPath GEO**: Mines and stratifies search queries into 5 intent categories (`commercial`, `compare`, `trust`, `solve`, `buy`), separating real observed user demand from exploration prompts.
🔹 **Hamzad AI Gateway**: Executes zero-secret multi-model inference across Gemini 2.5 Flash, GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and Perplexity Sonar.
🔹 **GEO-Scope**: Records full execution provenance (native vs fallback surrogate routes), measures brand mention frequencies, calculates Top-1 recommendation rates, and computes 95% bootstrap confidence intervals.
🔹 **MAVI (Molavi AI Visibility Index)**: Connects technical & entity audit diagnostics (SAGE L1–L4) with live empirical AI observation (GEO-Scope L5).

📊 The entire dataset, raw completions, and metrics package are 100% open-source and cryptographically verified with SHA-256 checksums.
